INREACH WX BOT
one inbound text each time. check-ins are free to send.

SETUP (do before you leave cell)
- bot gmail is the only recipient on all 3 check-ins. sync devices.
- tracking on, or do a message check after you send. device listens ~10 min after send, then ~hourly.
- first ever message to the bot (a check-in is fine) opens the reply thread.

6AM  (you send nothing)
  today's CALL at your last check-in GPS
  (pinned trip camp if you have never checked in; else the lat/lon fallback)
  one copy per MapShare name. checking in again does not add another 6am.

CHECK-INS
  okay / everything is okay     today's CALL
  starting my trip              NOW  next 3 hours
  ending my trip                TOM  tomorrow's CALL

CALL looks like
  EARLY 8/29 d2 47.21,-80.22
  SW20g38 6mm SHWR
  TSTM 15-19
  18/9

  line 1  GO | EARLY | SIT   then date   then dN lat,lon
  line 2  wind + rain + sky
  line 3  thunderstorm window if any, and/or EC alert
  last    hi/lo C

  GO     gusts <30, rain <8mm, no storms
  EARLY  gusts 30-39, or 8mm+, or storms today, or an alert
  SIT    gusts 40+, or 15mm+, or storms in travel hours with wind up

NOW looks like
  QUIET d2 47.15,-80.18 SW10 18c
    nothing happening. you still got a reply.
  NOW d2 ...
  14 SW18g30 17c
  15 SW22g36 16c TSTM
  16 SW20g40 16c 2mm

TOM is tomorrow's CALL (tonight's camp). tap it when you set camp.

IF NOTHING COMES BACK
  sky view. wait 10 min. send the same check-in again.
  Garmin Weather on the device still works (1 text, your GPS).
  type STOP to mute 6am. type GO to unmute.

SHORTHAND
  dN        trip day N (d1 = startDate). omitted when the point is GPS.
  47.21,-80.22   forecast point. last check-in GPS, else camp.

  SW20g38   wind from SW 20 km/h, gusts 38
  6mm       rain in that window
  18/9      high 18 C / low 9 C
  18c       air temp now-ish
  14-18     hours, 24h local (America/Toronto)
  15h       one hour

  CLR  clear          PC   partly cloudy     CLD  cloudy
  FOG  fog            DZ   drizzle
  SHWR showers        HVYSH heavy showers    HVY  heavy rain
  RAIN rain           TSTM thunderstorm      DWNPR downpour

  YEL/ORG/RED   EC alert colour
  warn / watch / statement   EC alert type

  +   we hit 160 chars and chopped the rest. still one message.

COST  (Essential = 50/month, send+receive)
  check-in out     free
  our reply        1 inbound
  6am push         1 inbound, you send nothing
  Garmin Weather   1 text, separate from this bot

TRIP  last check-in GPS is the forecast point. that is last night's camp
  if you tapped okay/end at the site.
  startDate in config is calendar day 1 of a pinned tripSlug, used only
  when there is no GPS yet. mark day-end camps in the paddle planner.
  null tripSlug does not auto-pick a park trip.
